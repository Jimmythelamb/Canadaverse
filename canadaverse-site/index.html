About the show

Honest, curious conversation.

Canada-Verse is a podcast that opens ideas and frees minds, with one goal: making humanity a better place. Hosted by Jimmy Lamb from North Vancouver, BC, each episode explores the stories, technology, and questions shaping Canada and the world, from news and innovation to the big ideas most people are afraid to ask about. No scripts or talking points, just honest, curious conversation.
